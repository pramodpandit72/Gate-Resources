# GATE CSE – Subject-wise Marks Weightage

## 1. GATE CSE Exam Overview

The GATE Computer Science and Information Technology (CSE) examination is conducted for a total of **100 marks** with a duration of **3 hours**.

| Section | Marks |
|---|---:|
| General Aptitude (GA) | 15 |
| Engineering Mathematics | 13–14 |
| Core Computer Science Subjects | 71–72 |
| **Total** | **100** |

## 2. Subject-wise Marks Weightage

The following table provides approximate subject-wise weightage based on historical GATE CSE question paper trends. Actual marks may vary every year.

| Subject | Approximate Marks | Priority |
|---|---:|---|
| General Aptitude | 15 | Very High |
| Engineering Mathematics | 13–14 | Very High |
| Programming & Data Structures | 10 | Very High |
| Computer Organization & Architecture (COA) | 9 | High |
| Operating Systems (OS) | 8–9 | High |
| Computer Networks (CN) | 8 | High |
| Algorithms | 8 | High |
| Theory of Computation (TOC) | 7–8 | High |
| Database Management Systems (DBMS) | 7–8 | High |
| Compiler Design | 6–7 | Medium |
| Digital Logic | 5–6 | Medium |

**Note:** These are approximate historical estimates, not guaranteed marks for the next GATE examination.

## 3. Subject Priority for Preparation

### Tier 1 – Highest Priority

Focus on these subjects first because they are important for building a strong foundation.

1. General Aptitude
2. Engineering Mathematics
3. Programming & Data Structures
4. Operating Systems
5. Computer Networks

### Tier 2 – High Priority

These subjects are also important and require thorough preparation.

1. Algorithms
2. Database Management Systems (DBMS)
3. Computer Organization & Architecture (COA)
4. Theory of Computation (TOC)

### Tier 3 – Complete After Tier 1 and Tier 2

Do not skip these subjects, as they can contribute valuable marks.

1. Digital Logic
2. Compiler Design

## 4. GATE CSE Examination Pattern

| Particulars | Details |
|---|---|
| Total Questions | 65 |
| Total Marks | 100 |
| Exam Duration | 3 Hours |
| 1-Mark Questions | 25 Marks |
| 2-Mark Questions | 75 Marks |
| Question Types | MCQ, MSQ, NAT |

### Negative Marking

| Question Type | Negative Marking |
|---|---|
| MCQ – 1 Mark | 1/3 mark |
| MCQ – 2 Marks | 2/3 mark |
| MSQ | No negative marking |
| NAT | No negative marking |

## 5. Recommended Subject Preparation Order

For beginners, the following sequence is recommended:

1. Discrete Mathematics
2. C Programming
3. Data Structures
4. Algorithms
5. Digital Logic
6. Computer Organization & Architecture
7. Operating Systems
8. Database Management Systems
9. Computer Networks
10. Theory of Computation
11. Compiler Design
12. Remaining Engineering Mathematics Topics
13. General Aptitude (Practice Regularly)

## 6. Recommended Preparation Strategy

- Solve at least 10 years of GATE Previous Year Questions (PYQs).
- Practice numerical problems daily.
- Prepare short notes for every subject.
- Revise completed subjects regularly.
- Take mock tests to improve speed and accuracy.
- Analyse mistakes after every mock test.
- Do not skip low-weightage subjects.
- Focus on conceptual clarity rather than memorising answers.
